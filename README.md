# 🎮 Gregory's Epic Adventure

An epic 2D pixel-art platformer game featuring Gregory, a brave Latino boy on an adventure across multiple worlds!

## ✨ Features

### 🌍 5 Unique Biomes
- **Grassland** - Classic platforming with green meadows and sunny skies
- **Underwater** - Slow-motion physics with bubbles and sea creatures
- **Desert** - Hot sands with scorpions and sandstorms
- **Space** - Low gravity environment with aliens and stars
- **Snow** - Slippery ice platforms with snowmen and falling snow

### 🎯 12 Challenging Levels
- Progressive difficulty across all biomes
- 2-3 levels per biome
- Final challenge levels combining the hardest obstacles

### 👾 Enemies to Defeat
- **Slimes** (Grassland) - Bouncy green creatures
- **Fish** (Underwater) - Swimming enemies
- **Scorpions** (Desert) - Dangerous desert dwellers
- **Aliens** (Space) - Extraterrestrial threats
- **Snowmen** (Snow) - Frosty opponents

Jump on enemies to defeat them and earn **50 points** each!

### 🌦️ Dynamic Weather Effects
- **Rain** (Grassland)
- **Bubbles** (Underwater)
- **Sandstorm** (Desert)
- **Stars** (Space)
- **Snowfall** (Snow)

### 💎 Power-ups
- **Shield** 🛡️ - Temporary invincibility (10 seconds)
- **Double Jump** 🪽 - Ability to jump twice in mid-air

### 🎨 Pixel Art Style
- Retro blocky graphics
- Vibrant Latino-inspired colors
- Smooth animations
- Crisp pixel-perfect rendering

### 🎵 Game Mechanics
- **Biome-specific physics** - Each world has unique gravity and movement
- **Coin collecting** - 100 points per coin
- **Enemy system** - Jump on enemies to defeat them
- **Lives system** - 3 lives to complete the adventure
- **Progressive scoring** - 500 points bonus per level completion

## 🎮 Controls

- **Arrow Keys** (← →) - Move left/right
- **Space Bar** or **Up Arrow** (↑) - Jump
- **Double Jump** - Jump again in mid-air (when power-up is collected)

## 🏆 Scoring System

- **Coins**: 100 points each
- **Defeating Enemies**: 50 points each
- **Power-ups**: 200 points each
- **Level Completion**: 500 points bonus

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Use arrow keys to move and space to jump
3. Collect coins and power-ups
4. Defeat enemies by jumping on them
5. Avoid getting hit by enemies (unless you have a shield!)
6. Reach the Mexican flag at the end of each level
7. Complete all 12 levels to win!
